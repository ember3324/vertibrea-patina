Crafting materials

Bigman's Rare Ores & Stones

Ingots graded 1 to 10. Low grades bring damage and brace. High grades take gems and magic. Grade 5 is balanced.

Grade bands

1–3Fortress: extreme damage and brace, own effects
4–5Military: strong, reliable
6–7Arcane: begins accepting gems
8–9Relic: highly receptive to enchantment
10Mythical: legendary power

Construction

Pure construction uses one material for full damage, brace and scaling. Composite mixes materials for more traits at lower base values. A staff is composite (staff + gem), and the staff's grade must allow the gem or it won't be powered.

Potions & brews

Tortia Defuego

A travelling fox merchant with a pack full of tinctures. Every brew lasts a few turns, so drink it right before you need it.

Bellahopstin's Bookstore
